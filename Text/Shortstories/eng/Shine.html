Shine

 

The October sun shone bright as if they had forgotten about the season that we were in. The river stood still almost as green as the grass all around. Only a couple of yellow leaves on top of it hinted at the season that we were in. No wind and no clouds - it was as if the summer had changed back into late spring. 

“This is supposed to be the spooky season, the season of storms and dark skies.” She sighed. The floor was not even moist. The road was full of bicycles. Nobody was even wearing jackets. She sat there and looked at the reflections in the river. Green trees in front of a blue sky, clear as a mirror. She was sure that if she were to get up and look into the river that she would be able to see the pores on her face. She had brought a bottle of lemonade. She opened it and looked inside. Purple liquid sparkled back at her.

As she looked up again, the road was suddenly empty. She realized only now that the water in the river had been moving before because now it was actually standing still. She stared at the surreality in front of her. Despite it still being hot she suddenly felt shivers creeping down her back, across her arms, over her thighs. Shivers all over her body. The shine of the sun somehow felt cold and hot at the same time. 

Then the river evolved. Subtle rings appeared in the center of it right in front of her. The rings grew bigger and turned into waves. There was something inside of the water. Cautiously she leaned forward, trying to catch a  glimpse of whatever was moving beyond the surface. She could not see any form. She could only presume a colour. She did not quite know what the colour was. Was it red? Was it purple? Was it blue? Was it black? She did not seem to know the colour. Then she realized that the sky had turned red. It was a dark red. It even seemed as if the texture had changed. Was the sky bleeding? 

She felt sickly. Her body rejected the air around her. It was thick and foul. It was a response of disgust. Not a mental disgust but a physical one. Mentally she felt anxiety and despair. What was going on around her? What has happened to this autumn noon? Forces that she could not grasp seemed to pull on her. She tried looking back at the river in front of her. Whatever was going on here seemed to originate in the waters. The water now also has changed its color to this mysterious shade and the waves now were bigger than horses riding away from its new center. Was it even a river at this point? Pressure. She felt small as an ant looking up at the elephant. It felt like her chest was about to burst out every second.

She tried to take a deep breath. She tried not to drown. But as she filled her lungs with the thick fullness, her stomach turned. She emptied her breakfast all over the grass. 

“Is everything okay with you?” Two cyclists have stopped their journey and looked at her with concern. From one moment to the next she was back in autumn. The sun shone bright again. The sky was blue and the river was still and brown. What was going on here? The old woman repeated her question to the girl and she replied only: “I think the fruits I had for breakfast have gone bad.” After the two cyclists had reassured themselves that she was doing fine now, they continued their journey.

After she was sitting by herself she dared to look one more time at the river. It was calm, slow and green. Whatever it was that was hiding inside of it, she now could not see it anymore.
