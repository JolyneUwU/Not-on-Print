Ne Zha

 

A little late to the party, but last year the movie “Ne Zha 2” made more than two billion US Dollars at the box offices. This not only made it a top 10 movie in terms of highest grossing movies, but also the highest grossing non Hollywood production and highest grossing animated movie. You never have heard of it? It is a Chinese movie that made its waves in Asian countries rather than in Western ones. Still is there not a little FOMO to have a movie that big and only few people in your circles know and talk about it? I took it as an opportunity. What is Ne Zha and is it worthwhile?

 

Ne Zha (2019)

Of course I would not start by watching the sequel to another movie. Ne Zha starts out rather simple: A super natural artifact gets split into two opposite powers and gets fed to two children: The emperor's son and the child of a dragon. Also there is a curse to kill the inhabitants of one of the pearls after three years… okay it's not super simple, but simple enough when you watch it. A mythological action adventure with two young demi gods in the center.

I do not think that it is the most nuanced or innovative story told in animation, but its embrace of Chinese mythology does give it a fresh spin from the perspective of a western audience. Still intuitive, it all makes a lot of sense and is easy to follow. So I would call the story “nothing too special but with a relatively fresh setting.”

The animation style is reminiscent of Pixar, but with a really high attention to detail and good sense of dynamics. The action is choreographed in a special way. So when I compare it to Pixar I have to say that it impressed me easily as much as the best Pixar productions. Overall I think there is something clean and plastic with the “pixar-look” that I personally am not a huge fan of in general, however you rarely see it executed that good.

The last thing I want to speak on is characters and tone over all. I don’t know what gets lost in translation of course, but just like the other aspects it all seemed quite familiar to me. It often is goofy and silly in the way that an all ages animated picture is expected to be. Then there are the serious and darker undertones, that never get to be too shocking or devastating. All the characters serve simple points and may (or may not have at all) one clear arc. Personally the point where it got frustrating was the showdown because that was where, aside from Pixar, another point of comparison made itself clear to me: The MCU. Less in the plot superstructure of cameos and cross references, but in the way they generically write a hero's origin story that has to end in that one big fight against a villain that is not too unsimilar to the protagonist. If you have watched a marvel origin story you might get the point. 

So what do we get? A very well executed but rather predictable all ages production. I would definitely prefer a lot of other movies from that genre. After the Pixar comparison I could mention movies like Soul and Inside Out. In terms of the overall field of animation though, I have to admit that it is a competent show of skill.

 

Ne Zha 2 (2025)

The second movie starts with a beautifully animated recap. So if my review of the first movie turned you off, just start with the second one. It is the more  interesting movie. You might not enjoy the dragons as much as I did without the context of the first movie. Outside of that there is not a lot to miss. 

I love talking about the spiderverse-effect. A movie that single handedly sparked studios to be more creative and out there with their animation styles. This can be said about Ne Zha 2  as well. The range of styles is massively widened. While the first one simply was a Pixar-Like, now we have additional aspects of realism, 2D animation and a Blizzard type videogame aesthetics. I don’t think it looks as consistently good as the first movie, but generally speaking I just prefer diversity over consistency. It is simply more interesting to look at. 

The plot picks up right where the first movie ended and therefore already leaves us with a more interesting premise. The cast we already know off finally gets some depth while also new and creative characters get introduced. The creativity really is the strength of the sequel. The scope of the story also evolves from that marvel-like plot to a genuine fantasy epic in the scope of a… dare I say it… Lord of the Rings. 

Part of this shift in tone also is a slight uptick in the violence that is shown in Ne Zha 2. To me it was a necessary side effect that came with the way the plot evolved. To others it might be an improvement, adding weight to the combat in Ne Zha 2. To the smallest of children it might be a little much. But take that with a grain of salt, I am neither a parent nor an expert on age ratings. 

The sequel to me is an all over improvement that I simply have to admire. The most important improvement is a subtle one though: They just give each scene a little more time than they did with the first. This gives characters, plot and themes necessarily more room to breath. I think all the qualities I enjoyed about Ne Zha 2 would not have had the same impact if they were presented with the pacing of the 2019 movie.

I can't help but think about the saying that an artist writes their first piece of work for the drawer. What is meant by that? Writing and creating art is such a complicated undertaking in order to learn how to make it well. You need to have made it for a first time. Jiao Zi has written and directed Ne Zha 1 and 2. Ne Zha was his debut as a writer and director for a feature film. Comparing the two movies feels like the first one was the learning experience necessary to make a movie like Ne Zha 2.

I really am impressed with the sequel and since this is the movie that broke all the records I have to say that I completely understand the hype around this franchise and want to recommend it.

 

Short: In the end I came to understand one of the biggest movies of all time. But I left mainly being impressed by the difference in quality between the first movie and second movie of an upcoming star of the world of animation.
