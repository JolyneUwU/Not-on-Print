Tex was laying in his bed. Eyes still open. This monster they were hunting was a fearsome creature. They call it the Rouge Puppet which means essentially it was just a young vampyr. A creature bitten and infected by a powerful parasite. A vamp, a creature that infects and controls the mind of its victims and in turn makes them stronger, quicker and immune to pain. That was essentially how Tex understood a vampyr, an aggressive, more dangerous version of another creature. This Vampyr is supposed to be a humanoid-Vampyr. Not quite a human, but the reports were unclear on that. Describing this Vampyr as ghoulish. It could be a wild human or a tallfoot or something entirely different. In any case it is dangerous reportedly preying on small animals and children but has also killed soldiers in the past. The executioners considered it to be a grade three danger to Order and gave Tex the responsibility to deal with this mess. A young priest that just had finished his education.

This part was what concerned him the most.

He was going through his plans over and over. He wanted to sleep, but he could not shake his concerns. He knew there was no big revelation coming to him and that being well rested would be way more crucial to the success of this mission but his head was just out of Order. He did not have a lot of battle experience and this mission seems to be way over his head. Why did the executioners pick him? He started to feel paranoid about what they knew and whether this mission was just an elaborate punishment for him.

Breath.

He went through all the possibilities and the best fate for him seemed to be that the mercenary is just that skilled but a little untrustworthy to the church. In that case all he has to do is just step aside and watch him do his job and prepare to properly report on anything that this man does. However this does not account for the presence of Niff and Naff on this mission. Would they be Tex’s bodyguards? A Plan B? The Church likes to be prepared for anything and so does Tex as well. If that was the Plan though why would he not be let in on that? Was the test not only on the mercenary's character but also on Tex himself? Every time he circles back to paranoia. What a foolish thing to presume. This puppet potentially was a real threat to the Order's realm, the church could not possibly risk anything here. Especially not to  just punish a low level priest. Or was he missing anything?

Door knocking.

“What is it?” Tex sat up as the Mercenary entered. “The girl from the inn has just vanished, I have reason to believe the puppet may be involved in this.” What? “Oh, really?” “Are you ready to get going?” This man… “Sure, I guess.” “Great, pack your things, get the others, I’ll be waiting in the front.” And just like that he already was out the door again. Tex buried his face in his hands. Why did he agree to that? He took a deep breath. “Put your robe back on, get your shoes, take a look around, grab any loose things you can find and then your backpack”. Tex was talking to himself like it helped him to be more efficient.

Breath.
