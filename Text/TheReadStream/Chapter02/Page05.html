“You two are Wyaar and Clayat, did I catch that right?” They just nodded. “So what brings you to the womb?” “Pilgrimage, we just finished our 14th milestone and want to become mature now." The Old Wanderer smiled under his Mask. Those two could not possibly still be pubescent. It was not the fact that they lied that made Alex smile, it was how bad their lies were. “Oh, wow, interesting… So what Milestone did you two just finish?” “We buried a family member?” “So you two are related?” “Yes.” “No!” Wyaar looked at Clayat, slightly shocked. Clayat tried to patch their falling lie together: “We come from the same town, Wyaar ended his first live stage by spending time with my family. But when crabfever hit our town two stars ago, we both lost a family member.” Smart. Clayat just implied a small lie - that Wyaars hypothetical Milestone of burying a family member, might be fake - to cover up their bigger lie. Those two were hilarious to Alex. She still did not quite understand what these two were onto, but they truly made the whole trip more interesting.

 

After that they followed the trails in silence for a while. The terrain was untamed and challenging. Not rarely they had to cut their way straight through thorny bushes. It is like the womb was actively fighting against its intruders. The trees grew taller and The trail got increasingly harder to follow at times they were only relying on the nose to hatch the direction in which this monstrum had taken Sim. No one else seemed to care; they just followed their leader. Was it out of laziness or inability to offer a reasonable rebuttal? It could not possibly have been out of trust, a currency the Old Wanderer could not fathom to have rightfully earned. Either way they did not particularly care. The focus was on hunting the prey and hunting the prey only. The crew was just dead weight being dragged along. Alex wondered whether their prey was climbing along the trees so that they would have a harder time following them or was it the Womb herself closing off those paths? Then they wondered whether it was possible that the kidnapper did not have a body at all. Just floating through the thicket. Maybe it was not the Rouge Puppet after all - a funny distraction they would have to explain to the priest. After endless years of wandering across the stream there still were lots of mysteries to Alex. This predator was something that they were going to face with curiosity and excitement. Like a small child catching its first grass hopper. A toy, a living being and a new experience at the same time. Was it excitement or just the tria-dust pumping through Alex’s veins?

They kept following the trails along thick trees, strange fruits and even thicker bushwork until Tex was crying out quietly: “I think it is time to take a break.”

The others looked hopefully at the Wanderer who did not realize that they were leading the pace. Apparently the group struggled to keep up with them but were just accepting their command. So Alex just nodded and said: “Everyone still keep your guard up though. This environment is hostile and I wonder how long it will take till we encounter some hostile fauna for the first time.” Good. Backpacks were unloaded, bushwork was cut down in a small circle, a fire was lit from it and food was brought out.
